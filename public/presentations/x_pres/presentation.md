# Intelligence Software Department — Operational Story Presentation

> **Visual Style Guide:** All scenes share a unified cinematic aesthetic — near-future military realism (circa 2028–2032), cool blue-teal color grading for command centers and technology, warm amber-orange tones for desert/field scenes, and high-contrast dramatic lighting throughout. UI screens use dark-mode interfaces with glowing neon-blue and cyan HUD elements. Think *a blend of Zero Dark Thirty meets Minority Report*.

---

## PART 1 — THE THREAT

### Scene 1: The Approaching Threat

**Context:** A lone hostile operative moves through rugged terrain under cover of twilight, approaching an Israeli military outpost. The tension is palpable — he believes he is undetected.

**Image Prompt:**
```
A cinematic wide-angle shot of a lone armed figure moving stealthily through rocky arid 
desert terrain at twilight. The sky is deep indigo and burnt orange at the horizon. The 
figure wears dark tactical clothing with a keffiyeh partially covering his face, carrying 
a worn backpack and a concealed weapon. In the far background, barely visible on a hilltop, 
sits a small fortified Israeli military outpost with dim perimeter lights and a 
communications antenna silhouetted against the sky. The terrain is scattered with dry 
shrubs and limestone boulders. Dust particles catch the last golden light. Shot from a low 
angle looking slightly upward, creating a menacing silhouette. Photorealistic, cinematic 
lighting, 8K detail, military thriller atmosphere, shallow depth of field on the background 
outpost. Color grading: warm amber shadows with cool blue highlights.
```

**Transition Prompt (1 → 2):**
```
A dramatic satellite orbital view looking down at a Middle Eastern desert landscape at 
twilight, with a single red pulsing target marker overlaid on the terrain. Thin digital 
scan lines sweep across the image like a surveillance system awakening. The curvature of 
the Earth is barely visible at the edges. A translucent holographic data overlay shows 
GPS coordinates, threat classification text reading "ALERT — UNIDENTIFIED HOSTILE 
MOVEMENT DETECTED", and a thin red trajectory line predicting the figure's path toward 
a marked friendly position. Photorealistic satellite imagery with futuristic military AR 
overlay, cool blue-cyan holographic elements on warm desert tones.
```

### Visual Continuity Notes
- **Drone design** should remain consistent across Scenes 4, 5, 7, 8, and 9 — angular stealth geometry, dark grey, blue thrust accents
- **Command center** environment should be recognizable as the same room in Scenes 2, 5, 8, and 10
- **UI software interfaces** should share a unified design language — dark themes, cyan/teal accents, glassmorphism panels, consistent typography
- **The operative** should appear as the same person across Scenes 1, 3 (surveillance photo), 7, and 9
- **Time of day** progresses: twilight (Scene 1) → full night (Scenes 7–9) → daytime office (Scenes 10–12)

### Recommended Image Dimensions
- Standard presentation slides: **1920 × 1080** (16:9)
- Detail/UI panels: **2560 × 1440** for maximum text legibility
- Cinematic scenes: **2560 × 1080** (21:9 ultrawide) for dramatic impact
